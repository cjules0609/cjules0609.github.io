Neutron star mergers · X-ray counterparts

From a merger to a measurable signal.

X-rays offer a window into the remnant of a neutron star merger. I model how the central engine, jet structure, and viewing geometry shape emission from the prompt phase through the afterglow.

Inside the research +

Different merger outcomes—from a long-lived magnetar to a black hole—can produce distinct X-ray signatures. Jointly modeling prompt emission and afterglows helps constrain geometry and identify useful windows for follow-up observations.

PromptX is my open-source Python framework for modeling prompt radiation from relativistic outflows. It connects the underlying geometry to observer-frame light curves and spectra.

Einstein Probe · Fast X-ray transients

Beyond a change in perspective.

Are fast X-ray transients ordinary gamma-ray bursts seen from another angle? My work tests that idea and finds that standard viewing-angle effects alone cannot explain some of these unusually soft events.

Inside the research +

Using simulated populations of single- and multi-component jets, I compare predicted energetics with observations. Some Einstein Probe transients occupy a region of parameter space that calls for different outflow properties or radiation physics, beyond the standard off-axis interpretation.

Magnetars · Fast radio bursts

What lies outside the beam?

A burst can look dramatically different away from its emission cone. I use radio and X-ray observations of the Galactic magnetar SGR J1935+2154 to constrain the geometry and relativistic beaming of fast radio bursts.

Inside the research +

By comparing bright fast radio bursts with possible off-axis “slow radio bursts,” we developed closure relations and tested beaming constraints under different assumptions about their association with X-ray bursts. This links observed burst properties to source geometry and relativistic motion.

The tools behind the science

VegasAfterglow

A high-performance C++ framework with Python support for modeling gamma-ray burst afterglows. Developed with Dr. Yihan Wang and Dr. Bing Zhang, it connects flexible jet and environment models to broadband observations.

Code & documentation ↗Paper on ADS ↗

PromptX

A Python framework for modeling prompt X-ray emission from neutron star mergers. PromptX computes light curves and spectra for structured relativistic jets and magnetar-powered winds, viewed from arbitrary angles.
