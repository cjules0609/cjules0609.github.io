About me

A little about me.

I’m a Postdoctoral Researcher at Louisiana State University, working in high-energy, time-domain, and multimessenger astrophysics. Since August 2026, I’ve worked with Dr. Eric Burns in the LSU High-Energy Astrophysics group and as part of the COSI collaboration. My current work includes integrated transient discovery and classification pipelines, scientific software, and interdisciplinary research spanning AI, medical physics, and astrophysics.

I earned my PhD in Astronomy at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas in 2026, advised by Dr. Bing Zhang. My doctoral dissertation explored radiation processes and geometric effects in relativistic transients.

At Santa Clara University, I earned a B.S. in Physics with a concentration in Astrophysics and a minor in Mathematics. During my studies, I worked with Dr. Kristin Kulas and Dr. B-G Andersson at the SOFIA Science Center at NASA Ames to study dust polarization in the interstellar medium through radiative alignment torques.

In my free time, I enjoy weightlifting, Muay Thai, singing, riding motorcycles, hiking, and spending time with my two orange cats and two Shih Tzu–Chihuahua mixed dogs. I’m also usually up for watching—or playing—football and basketball. My favorite music includes EDM (house, techno, and sometimes dubstep), rap, and R&B.
